🎨 Doodle Deck

Creative Tools for Kids to Learn and Play!

✏️

Text Editor

Create colorful text with fun fonts and amazing themes! Watch your words come alive with rainbow, jungle, sunset, and galaxy effects.

🎨

Color Mixer

Mix colors together and discover new ones! Drag and drop colors into the mixing bowl and learn about color combinations.

🖌️

Paint Canvas

Create amazing artwork with brushes, rollers, stamps, and splash effects! Choose your colors and let your imagination run wild.

✨

Magic Particles

Use your hands to control magical floating shapes! Make stars, hearts, and flowers dance in 3D.

🔤

Alphabet Adventure

Spin the pangram wheel and learn to write all 26 letters! Watch letters glow as you practice writing the alphabet.

🔢

Number Bingo

Learn numbers with a fun bingo game! Spin the carousel, hear numbers, and practice writing them.
